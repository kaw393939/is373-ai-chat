Reviewed the complete GoDaddy zone through Computer Use in native Safari. There were 16 records before the change and 17 afterward. This document records the observation and rationale; GoDaddy remains authoritative for live DNS.

| Host | Type | Destination | TTL | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| @ | A | 159.203.182.71 | 600 seconds | Existing; preserved |
| dev | A | 159.203.182.71 | 600 seconds | Existing; preserved |
| qa | A | 159.203.182.71 | 600 seconds | Added; verified at both authoritative servers |
| www, traefik | A | 159.203.182.71 | 600 seconds | Existing legacy routes; preserved |

Authoritative servers are ns15.domaincontrol.com and ns16.domaincontrol.com. The five Google MX records retain priorities 1, 5, 5, 10 and 10 and TTL 3600. Google site verification, SOA, nameserver and `_domainconnect` records were preserved.

The existing root SPF delegates to `dc-aa8e722993._spfm.firehose360.com`, whose TXT includes `_spf.google.com`. That indirection is valid and may be provider-managed; flattening or deleting it offers no established benefit here. No duplicate root SPF was added.

No DKIM selector was visible in the zone and no `_dmarc` TXT, CAA, AAAA or DS answer was observed during this audit. Absence of a visible DKIM record does not establish whether Workspace currently signs messages. Issue #3 owns verification of Google signing, Resend sending-domain DKIM/SPF and alignment before tightening DMARC policy. Existing receiving mail must keep its Google MX; an app sending subdomain does not require buying another Workspace mailbox. No email-authentication policy change was made in this DNS slice.

DNS chooses an IP; Traefik chooses the app and issues TLS certificates. Adding `qa` alone therefore does not deploy QA. At the first routing check, dev served the old Apache site and QA lacked its certificate. Deployment evidence belongs in the [environment runbook](../environments.md), rather than being inferred from DNS.

Recheck with `dig @ns15.domaincontrol.com qa.firehose360.com A` and the equivalent ns16 query. HTTPS checks must verify certificates normally. Keep the 600-second A-record TTL while routing is changing; raising it later is optional, with a slower rollback tradeoff.

Reference: [GoDaddy A-record fields](https://www.godaddy.com/help/add-an-a-record-19238).
