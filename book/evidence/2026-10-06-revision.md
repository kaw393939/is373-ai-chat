who did not author these materials must follow the sample labs, record confusion
and outcomes, and have the resulting revisions verified before classroom
validation can be claimed. AI editorial reviewers and passing commands cannot
provide that evidence. No commercial publisher acceptance, adoption demand or
print/PDF layout proof is asserted by this development edition.

scanning, push protection and private vulnerability reporting were enabled; the
secret-scanning alert list was empty at the recorded check.
